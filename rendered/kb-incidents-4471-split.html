The problems

  • parser: the shared parser flips day and month (#1). It reads every date month first, and our tenants write the day first. Booked 7 April, the invite says 4 July. Every tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is the fix that is queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first (#2). It prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field; people type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The same flip, a different bug: only reports sent through the v3 form, and the parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day move: dates move by one day (#3). Not a flip. The same meeting shows on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made; “every booking in June is one day off”. The desk already read these as a one-day move and counted them under parser by tenant. They need their own fix, most likely in how invite times are converted between time zones.

Why it was one ticket

Duplicates are merged on subject line (decision 0001), and subject lines don’t tell these problems apart: “Date is wrong” and “Month and day swapped?” come in under every one of them. The subject says the date is wrong; only the body and the tenant say why. Intake tags are a browser guess; counts here are by tenant.

Shipping the parser fix and closing #4471 leaves #2 and #3 open.
